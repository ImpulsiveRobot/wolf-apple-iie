FIELD MANUAL — CASTLE WOLFENSTEIN (1981)

You are an Allied prisoner escaping from the 3-floor fortress of Castle Wolfenstein. Locate the Secret War Plans in the Floor 3 War Room Vault, then fight or sneak your way out through the Floor 3 Castle Exit Gate.

Modern Controls

  • WASD — Move in 8 directions
  • Mouse or Arrows — Aim pistol
  • Left Click or Space — Fire pistol
  • Right Click or G — Throw grenade
  • E or F — Open chest / Frisk / Search / Unlock
  • R — Holster or draw pistol
  • M — Toggle Floor Map

Classic Apple IIe Controls

  • QWE / AD / ZXC — 8-way move (S stop)
  • IJKL or Arrows — Aim pistol
  • Space — Fire pistol
  • T — Throw grenade
  • U — Use / Open chest / Frisk / Unlock
  • H — Holster or draw pistol
  • M — Toggle Floor Map

Classic Survival Tactics

  • Hold Up Guards: Sneak close behind or beside an unalerted regular guard and aim your pistol at them. They raise their hands and shout “NEIN!” — walk up and press E/U to frisk them for bullets, grenades, or keys! (Even an empty gun works as a bluff!)
  • Disguise & Holster: Find a Guard Uniform and press R/H to holster your gun. Regular guards let you walk right past unless you open chests in front of them or bump into them.
  • Beware SS Stormtroopers: Violet/armored SS guards always see through uniforms, wear bulletproof vests (3+ pistol shots or 1 grenade), and will chase you into adjacent rooms!
  • Locked Doors: Open with a Brass Key (instant), pick the lock with E/U (takes ~2.5s), or blow the door apart with a Grenade.
  • Chests: Hold still while cracking a chest open to discover Ammo, Grenades, Uniforms, Bulletproof Vests, Bratwurst (+1 HP), Schnapps (wobbly aim!), or the Secret War Plans.
